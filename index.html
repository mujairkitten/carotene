About Carotene

What this is

A home for small web tools. Each one opens in its own page and keeps its data in your browser.

Tools

Track Record — v5.0.5
'Checklist' — Beta 2

More are on the way.

Data

Character aptitudes and race data are compiled from various sources, including community wikis and official Global sources.

The race calendar covers G1–G3 races across Junior, Classic, and Senior year groups.

Privacy & backup

Each tool keeps its data private in this browser. No accounts, no server, no tracking — your information never leaves your device.

Track Record offers Backup/Import to move your list between sessions or devices. Checklist stores its tasks locally as well.

The only network request any page makes is loading its font from Google Fonts.
