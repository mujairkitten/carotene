About 'Checklist'

VersionBeta 1
How it works

Tick a task when you finish it. Daily tasks untick at 15:00 UTC and Content tasks untick at 22:00 UTC. One-time tasks stay until you untick or remove them.

The seven required daily missions can't be removed. Ticking Complete Daily Missions ticks all of them, and you can add extra or event missions under it.

Ticking Daily Race (3 times) also ticks Daily Race (once), and ticking Request the shoes in the Club also ticks Check the Club. Unticking the first task of a pair unticks the second one too.

Each list shows a live countdown to its next reset, and that time in your own time zone.

Privacy

Your tasks stay private in this browser. Clearing your browser data will erase them.
